Why 3000×3000 is the safe choice

The same cover is shown as a tiny thumbnail on a phone and full screen on a TV. A 3000×3000 file looks sharp everywhere and meets the strictest requirement (Apple Music), so you never have to export different sizes.

Pixels matter, not DPI

For streaming, only the pixel size counts. A 3000×3000 image at 72 or 300 DPI is the same file for Spotify. If a distributor asks for DPI, 72–300 is fine.

Don't send the cover through WhatsApp

WhatsApp, Telegram and similar apps compress and shrink images, so your 3000×3000 cover arrives smaller and blurry. Move it as a file: email, Google Drive or a USB cable.

FAQ

What size is Spotify cover art?

Spotify accepts square images from 640 to 10,000 px and asks for the highest resolution available. In practice, 3000×3000 px is the standard that every distributor accepts.

My image is smaller than 3000 px. Can I use it?

StreamPack upscales it to 3000×3000 and warns you if it may look blurry. Apple Music can reject covers that look low quality, so start from the largest image you have.

JPG or PNG?

Both are accepted. JPG keeps the file well under 10 MB, which avoids upload errors.
