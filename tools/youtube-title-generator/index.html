FAQ
How long should a YouTube title be?
YouTube allows up to 100 characters, but long titles get cut off in search results and on mobile. Keep the important words in the first 60-70 characters; this tool aims for titles under 70.
Where should the keyword go?
Near the start, written the way people search for it. Then add the reason to click: a number, a result, a mistake to avoid or a question. The title, thumbnail and first seconds of the video should all promise the same thing.
