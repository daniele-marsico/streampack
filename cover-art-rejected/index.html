Checklist before you upload

  • Square (1:1) image: no rectangles, no white borders added to fake a square
  • 3000×3000 px, JPG or PNG, under 10 MB
  • RGB colour, not CMYK (print files are often CMYK)
  • Any text must match the release: artist name and title exactly as in your metadata
  • No website URLs, @handles or social media logos
  • No prices, "new", "exclusive" or store logos (Spotify, Apple, Amazon…)
  • No images you don't have the rights to
  • Not blurry or pixelated: don't upscale a tiny image

What StreamPack fixes for you

FAQ

Why was my cover art rejected by DistroKid?

The most common reasons are a size or format problem (not square, too small, CMYK), text that doesn't match the artist name or title, and forbidden elements such as URLs, @handles or store logos.

Can I use an AI-generated cover?

Many distributors accept original AI-generated artwork as long as you have the right to use it. Check your distributor's current rules before releasing.

How long does it take after I fix it?

It depends on your distributor: you upload the corrected cover and the release goes back into review.
