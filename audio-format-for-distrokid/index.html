When you need StreamPack and when you don't

If your DAW already exported a WAV or FLAC master that your distributor accepts, upload it as it is: Spotify asks for the native master and doesn't want it downsampled. StreamPack is for the other cases: the file is in a format the distributor refuses, it's 48 kHz or mono, or you're not sure. It gives you a stereo 44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV or FLAC that every distributor takes.

File names

DistroKid refuses file names containing \ / : * ? " ' < > |. StreamPack names the files with your artist name and the title, without those characters.

FAQ

WAV or FLAC for DistroKid?

Both are lossless, so the sound is identical. FLAC files are smaller, which helps with long tracks or albums.

Is MP3 enough?

Some distributors accept MP3, but lossless WAV or FLAC is recommended: the platforms re-encode your file, and starting from MP3 loses more quality.

Should I upload 24-bit?

If your master is 24-bit and your distributor accepts it, yes. Amuse only takes 16-bit; when in doubt, 16-bit 44.1 kHz is accepted everywhere.
