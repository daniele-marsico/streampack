FAQ

Is it free to convert WAV to FLAC?

Yes, StreamPack is free with no sign-up and no watermark.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion runs on your device, in the browser or in the Android app, so your unreleased music stays private.

What FLAC settings does it use?

FLAC 16-bit 44.1 kHz (lossless), stereo. These are the settings DistroKid, Spotify and most distributors accept. You can also normalize loudness to about -14 LUFS.
